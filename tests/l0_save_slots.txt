# The save list names a save's scenario by the registry entry of its id and sandbox flag, not by the digits in the id: the mod's
# campaign puts s2 first, so scenario 2 of the menu is s1, and its saves (as the campaign scenario, then as the sandbox of the
# same slot) must list as menu slot 1, with the day, time, cash, guests and title of the running game.
# @env OBL_PACK=l0
# @env OBL_MODS=l0_campaign_order
# @timeout 120
seed 1
fixed-dt 0.02
boot-gameplay scenario 2
await gameplay
call-drive ui.save-game-list-written 2
call-report ui.save-slots-fixture
expect-last calls 2
quit
