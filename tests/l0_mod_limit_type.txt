# The per-type limit of CreateObject (docs/modding/testing.md#limits): helpers stay in the resort, so a new day starts the daily count
# again but the type's count goes on. One visitor makes about 16 helpers a day, under the daily limit, and the 41st helper of
# the type stops the game on a later day.
# @env OBL_PACK=l0
# @env OBL_MODS=l0_limits
# @timeout 240
# @expect-exit 1
# @expect-log FatalError: .*Function CreateObject: the limit of 40 objects of the type "limit_helper"
seed 1
fixed-dt 0.02
boot-gameplay scenario 1
await gameplay
world-script-change STATE_WORLD_AFTERGAME
command set-game-speed level=2
step 1
spawn-guests 1
step 2000
expect day 0
step 1000
expect day 1
step 3200
expect day 2
step 8000
quit
