# The generic object functions that create, find and change things (l0_object_fn): CreateObject, RemoveObject, ChargeMoney, PayMoney,
# FindNearestCommodity, ChangeGuestsDesire. Makers create a helper that charges 2 and removes itself, look for a drinks building and raise the
# snack desire of the guests around them.
# @env OBL_PACK=l0
# @env OBL_MODS=l0_object_fn
# @timeout 180
seed 1
fixed-dt 0.02
boot-gameplay scenario 1
await gameplay
step 50
spawn-building kiosk 60 60
spawn-guests 2
expect guests 4
step 100
expect guests 2
log make guests=$guests income=$income-today costs=$costs-today
expect income-today 8
expect costs-today 6
step 3000
quit
