# The player's `[Keys]` binding (docs/modding/reference/actions.md): `bind-key` does what a line of openbeach.ini does. game.pause moves
# from P to F10; "default" gives the key back. SDL scancodes: P 19, F10 67.
# @env OBL_PACK=l0
# @timeout 120
seed 1
fixed-dt 0.02
boot-gameplay scenario 1
await gameplay
step 5
bind-key game.pause F10
sdl-key 19 1
step 3
sdl-key 19 0
step 3
expect paused 0
sdl-key 67 1
step 3
sdl-key 67 0
step 3
expect paused 1
sdl-key 67 1
step 3
sdl-key 67 0
step 3
expect paused 0
bind-key game.pause default
sdl-key 19 1
step 3
sdl-key 19 0
step 3
expect paused 1
quit
